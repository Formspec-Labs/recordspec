This certificate describes the selected case snapshot. The offline verifier checks the retained bytes, attachment binding and signing references. Workflow completion and disclosure permission come from the selected product. Operator signatures are checked when this certificate is issued. Offline operator verification requires a separately selected, independently trusted public-key registry; without it operator authority remains unchecked.
